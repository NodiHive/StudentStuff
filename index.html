M & M can make perfect brats in an Oven — Easy Peasy

  • Preheat to 400°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
  • Arrange the brats with a little space between them, and bake 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  • Don't prick the casings — that's a myth. Piercing them just lets the juices escape and dries them out.
  • Check doneness without a thermometer: the casing should be browned and blistered, and when you cut one open, the inside should be firm (not squishy or translucent) and the juices should run clear, not pink.
  • For extra snap on the casing, broil for the last 2–3 minutes, watching closely so they don't scorch.
  • Optional flavor boost: after baking, toss them in a skillet with sliced onions and a splash of beer over medium heat for a few minutes.
No need for parboiling or extra steps — the oven method alone gets you fully cooked, evenly browned brats.
